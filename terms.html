Last updated 6 September 2026 · ExpoWalk is run by Akhil Mittal, Ambala, India · info@expowalk.app

ExpoWalk is a free planning tool for visitors to trade shows such as IMTS 2026. By using it you agree to these short terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. What you get

A browser app to plan booth visits, walk the halls with a map, record booth conversations, scan visiting cards and paper notes, and have them turned into text by Google's Gemini AI — either with your own Google API key or, for the first 100 signed-in users who agree to be contacted, with a limited free allowance paid for by ExpoWalk. The allowance (currently 25 recordings, 300 scans, 8 hours of audio per user, within an overall budget) can be reduced, paused or withdrawn at any time without notice, for example when the budget is used up.

2. Your data and your responsibility

Recordings, photos, transcripts and minutes stay on your device and never reach ExpoWalk; see the privacy page. That also means you are responsible for keeping them safe — clearing your browser data, losing your phone or uninstalling the browser deletes them. Export or email what you want to keep.

You must obtain the consent of everyone you record where the law requires it (Illinois, where IMTS is held, is an all-party-consent state), and you must not use the app to scan, store or process anyone's personal data unlawfully.

Exhibitor names, booth numbers, categories and short descriptions are compiled from the public IMTS 2026 exhibitor directory (© AMT – The Association For Manufacturing Technology) and hall layouts are redrawn from the official floor plan, for your personal visit planning only. They may be incomplete or out of date; always check the official directory at directory.imts.com, to which every exhibitor in the app links. ExpoWalk is an independent visitor tool and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by AMT or IMTS; IMTS is a trademark of AMT. If you are the rights holder of any content shown and want it changed or removed, email info@expowalk.app and we will act within 48 hours.

Transcripts, minutes and card details are produced automatically by an AI model and can contain mistakes — wrong names, numbers or commitments. Check anything important against your own notes before acting on it.
