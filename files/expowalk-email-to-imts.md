# Courtesy notice to IMTS / AMT

**To:** info@imts.com
**Cc:** amtwebmaster@amtonline.org
**From:** info@expowalk.app
**Subject:** Free visitor planning tool for IMTS 2026 (ExpoWalk) — courtesy notice and attribution

Dear IMTS team,

I am a regular IMTS visitor from India (aerospace and precision-machining supply chain) and will be at McCormick Place from 14 to 19 September. Ahead of the show I built a small, free web tool for visitors like me, and I want to let you know about it before I share it more widely, and confirm that the way it uses the public directory is acceptable to you.

**What it is:** ExpoWalk (https://expowalk.app) is a browser app that lets a visitor shortlist exhibitors, see their booths on the hall map, plan each day's walk, and keep their own notes, visiting-card photos and booth-conversation recordings on their phone. It is free, has no advertising, and I do not sell it or any data.

**How it uses IMTS material:**
- Exhibitor names, booth numbers, halls, categories and a short (≤240-character) excerpt of each exhibitor's public description, taken from the public IMTS 2026 exhibitor directory on 5 September 2026.
- Hall layouts redrawn from the official floor plan so that a visitor's shortlist can be shown on a map.
- Every exhibitor entry links to its official page on directory.imts.com, and the app tells users to verify booth details there.
- The app and site carry the notice: "ExpoWalk is an independent visitor tool, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by AMT or IMTS. IMTS is a trademark of AMT. Exhibitor data comes from the public IMTS 2026 directory." The IMTS logo is not used.
- No attendee or exhibitor contact data is collected from IMTS; the only personal data the tool stores is what a user chooses to enter about themselves.

I believe this is a reasonable, attributed use of publicly listed information that sends visitors to the official directory rather than away from it. If you would prefer a different attribution wording, a different link format, or the removal of any element (for example the description excerpts or the redrawn hall layouts), tell me and I will make the change within 48 hours. Equally, if you would like to see it or discuss making something like it available to visitors in a more official way for future shows, I would welcome that conversation.

Thank you for a show that is worth flying halfway around the world for.
