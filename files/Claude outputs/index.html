How it works

Four steps. The first two happen on a laptop before you fly; the last two on your phone.

01 · BEFORE THE SHOW

Shortlist

Search all 1,752 exhibitors by name, product category, country or hall. Mark each one 1–5 stars, add a note on what to ask, and share the list with your team or dealers.

02 · PLAN

Plan each day

Assign booths to days, and the planner orders them hall by hall into a walk list with a route. Print an A4 pack of the maps with your booths highlighted as a paper fallback.

03 · AT THE SHOW

Walk, record, scan

The hall map shows where you are and the next booth. At each stand, record the conversation (with the exhibitor's consent), photograph the visiting card and your paper notes. All saved on the phone — no network needed.

04 · SAME EVENING

Get the minutes

Back at the hotel, press Process: the phone sends each recording straight to Google's Gemini and gets back a transcript, minutes, next actions and the card details — grouped per exhibitor, ready to share or email.

What you get

Full exhibitor directory

Every listed exhibitor with booth, hall, pavilion, categories, country and website, filterable in a second — no more paging through the official app.

Hall maps that know where you are

Booth outlines for all five halls, GPS position, aisle-aware routing between your stops, toilets and exits marked.

Stars, notes and day plans

1–5 star priority, private notes, a day planner and a walk list ordered by hall and aisle.

Offline booth recording

One tap to record, saved to the phone only. Nothing is uploaded until you decide to process it.

Cards and paper notes

Photograph visiting cards and handwritten notes per exhibitor; AI reads them into contact records and text. Export as vCard or CSV.

Minutes you can send

Transcript, minutes and next actions per meeting, viewable in the app, shareable by WhatsApp, or emailed to yourself.

Your conversations never reach us

ExpoWalk was built by a trade-show visitor who did not want his supplier conversations on someone else's server. So it has none.

  • Recordings, photos, transcripts and minutes live only on your device. The app has no upload path for them.
  • Processing goes from your phone directly to Google's Gemini API (paid tier, not used for training). ExpoWalk's server only hands your phone a one-hour permission token and counts how many you used — it never sees the content.
  • The only things we store are the sign-in details you choose to give us (name, company, email, phone) and usage counters — and only if you sign in.
  • The code is open source, so anyone can verify this: github.com/mittalakhil87/imts-planner.

Everything except the paid-for AI works without an account: directory, maps, stars, day plans, recording and scanning. For the AI, get a free Gemini API key from Google (takes two minutes) and paste it under Meetings → Settings in the app — then no ExpoWalk server is involved in anything you do.

Recording other people

Illinois, like many places, requires everyone in a private conversation to agree to being recorded. The app reminds you before each recording; ask the person at the booth first — most say yes when they hear you'll get accurate minutes of what they promised.
