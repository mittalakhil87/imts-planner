# ExpoWalk launch messages

## 1. LinkedIn post (personal page)

Going to IMTS in Chicago next week? I built a small tool for exactly that trip, and I'm opening it to the first 100 visitors for free.

It's called ExpoWalk. It's a web app, nothing to install from a store, and it does the four things I always wished the official show app did:

1. The complete exhibitor list (1,752 companies) that you can search by product, country or hall and mark 1–5 stars, on your laptop, before you fly.
2. Real hall maps for all five halls, with your starred booths highlighted, GPS position and an aisle-by-aisle walk list per day. There is even an A4 print pack for when the battery dies.
3. At each booth: one tap to record the conversation, one tap to photograph the visiting card and your paper notes. All of it is stored on your phone only — nothing uploads while you walk.
4. Back at the hotel, press Process and get a transcript, minutes, next actions and the card details per exhibitor, ready to share or email.

The part I care most about: your recordings never touch my server. Processing goes from your phone straight to Google's Gemini, and the code is open source so anyone can check that claim.

For the first 100 people who sign up, ExpoWalk pays for the AI processing — 25 recordings and 300 card scans each. No credit card.

👉 expowalk.app

To keep it on your home screen like an app: open expowalk.app/imts2026, then Chrome ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" (Android) or Safari Share → "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone), and always open it from the icon.

I'll be walking the halls with it from Sept 14. If you're there, tell me what breaks and what you'd want next.

*ExpoWalk – free planner for IMTS 2026, Chicago (14–19 Sept)*

🔒 Recordings never reach any server of mine – phone → Google AI directly. Code is open source.

🔗 https://expowalk.app

*Install it like an app (2 minutes, do this on the phone you'll carry):*

📱 *Android (Chrome):*
1. Open https://expowalk.app/imts2026 in Chrome
2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → *Add to Home screen* (or *Install app*) → Add
3. Open ExpoWalk from the new icon. Tap the name at the top right → Sign in (Google or email) → fill your name & company once → free allowance is active
   (Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO / some Samsung phones: if nothing appears on the home screen, allow it once: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Other permissions → *Home screen shortcuts* → Allow, then repeat step 2.)

🍎 *iPhone (Safari):*
1. Open https://expowalk.app/imts2026 in Safari (not Chrome)
2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) → *Add to Home Screen* → Add
3. Open ExpoWalk from the icon and sign in from inside it (top right → Continue with Google)

⚠️ Always use it from the icon after installing – on iPhone, recordings made in Safari and in the installed app are stored separately.

💻 On a laptop, just open https://expowalk.app/imts2026 in Chrome – do your starring and day planning there, then Plan → *Copy plan code* and paste it into the phone (WhatsApp it to yourself). Stars, notes and days all travel with the code.
