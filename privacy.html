ExpoWalk — how your data is handled

Last updated 6 September 2026 · ExpoWalk is run by Akhil Mittal, Ambala, India · info@expowalk.app

ExpoWalk is a trade-show planner that runs in your browser. It was built on one principle: what you record at a booth belongs to you and never reaches us.

Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google, to identify your account. We request no other Google data and never access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.

What we do not do

No analytics or advertising trackers. No selling or sharing of personal data. No access to your recordings, photos, transcripts or minutes.

Your rights

Email info@expowalk.app to see, correct or delete your account details or to withdraw consent; we act within 7 days. Everything stored on your device you control directly.

Recording others

Many places, including Illinois (IMTS), require every participant's consent to record a private conversation. The app asks you to confirm consent before each recording; obtaining it is your responsibility.

Open source

The complete code is public at github.com/mittalakhil87/imts-planner, so anyone can verify these statements.
