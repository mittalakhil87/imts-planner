02 · How it works

Four steps.
Two before you fly.

Shortlisting and planning happen on a laptop at your desk. Walking, recording and scanning happen on your phone. The minutes arrive the same evening.

1
Before the show

Shortlist

Search all 1,752 exhibitors by name, product category, country or hall. Mark each one 1–5 stars, add a note on what to ask, and share the list with colleagues or dealers.

2
Plan

Plan each day

Assign booths to days; the planner orders them hall by hall into a walk list with a route. Print an A4 pack of the maps with your booths highlighted as the paper fallback.

3
At the show

Walk, record, scan

The hall map shows where you are and the next booth. At each stand, record the conversation (with the exhibitor's consent), photograph the visiting card and your notes. Everything is saved on the phone — no network needed.

4
Same evening

Get the minutes

Back at the hotel, press Process: your phone sends each recording straight to Google's Gemini and gets back a transcript, minutes, next actions and the card details — grouped per exhibitor, ready to share or email.

04 · Privacy
Zero.

Conversations that never reach us

ExpoWalk was built by a trade-show visitor who did not want his supplier conversations on someone else's server. So the app has none. This is architecture, not a promise.

  • [✓]Recordings, photos, transcripts and minutes live only on your device. The app has no upload path for them.
  • [✓]Processing goes from your phone directly to Google's Gemini API (paid tier, not used for training). ExpoWalk's server only hands your phone a one-hour permission token and counts how many you used — it never sees the content.
  • [✓]The only things we store are the details you choose to give us when you sign in (name, company, email, phone) and usage counters.
  • [✓]The code is open source, so anyone can verify this: github.com/mittalakhil87/imts-planner.

Recording other people

Ask first. It works.

Illinois, like many places, requires everyone in a private conversation to agree to being recorded. The app reminds you before each recording. Most exhibitors say yes when they hear you'll get accurate minutes of what they promised.

Put it on your home screen

Works like an app. No store.

Android (Chrome): open expowalk.app/imts2026 → ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or "Install app"). iPhone (Safari): open the same link → Share button → Add to Home Screen. Then always open it from the icon — recordings and notes are stored inside that app.

Any phone, any country

Runs in the browser you already have — Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone, anything on a laptop. No app store, no account needed to start, and the exhibitor directory covers companies from more than 30 countries.
